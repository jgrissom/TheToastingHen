Allergen Information

No Peanut or Tree Nut Ingredients.

We use no peanut or tree nut ingredients in our recipes or at our mobile canopy setup. Some items, including our bread, come from outside suppliers and may be made where nuts are handled. Our menu is prepared in a licensed shared commercial commissary that may also process peanuts and tree nuts from other kitchen members. We clean and sanitize our equipment, but cross-contact remains possible.
